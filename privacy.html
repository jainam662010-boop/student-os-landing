Deep Lock

A shield, not a surveillance app.

The Focus Shield notices when you leave a session and brings you back. It never reads screen content, messages, passwords or typed text — by design, not by promise alone.

  • Zero content access — only app-level detection
  • Zero network calls during a session
  • You control every permission and every blocked app
