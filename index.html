Six modules · one app · no account

Designed to help you study more with less stress.

Student OS is for anyone juggling classes, exams and self-study who wants one calm place to run all of it.

Smart planner

Plan My Day, move tasks when plans change, and get recovery suggestions after a missed session. Sleep settings keep the schedule honest.

Focus timer with Deep Lock

Start a focus block of 30 to 120 minutes. Deep Lock is optional and blocks the apps you choose while the timer runs.

Routines and streaks

Morning study, exercise, review notes. Check habits off daily and watch your best streak grow.

Also on iPhone

Your study day, now on iPhone.

Student OS is a home-screen web app, so there is no App Store download. Open the link in Safari, add it to your home screen, and it launches full screen, works offline and keeps your data on the device.

  1. 1
    Open the link in Safari

    Not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or another app’s browser — those cannot add apps to the home screen.

  2. 2
    Tap Share

    The square with an arrow, in Safari’s toolbar.

  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen, then Add

    Student OS gets its own icon, splash screen and offline cache.
